01 — frequencies

Six live tracks. Pick a signal, follow it down.

Every track runs the full three days — its own mentors, its own bench of hardware and compute, its own bracket. Drag the network above to see how they connect.

Node 01

AI / ML Lab

Neural systems, computer vision, and generative models — bring a dataset or build one live. GPU bench on site.

Node 02

Robotics & Embedded

Autonomous bots, sensor fusion, and firmware under a clock. The arena qualifiers run on day one.

Node 03

Web3 & Systems

Protocols, distributed infrastructure, and contracts that have to survive contact with real users.

Node 04

Design & Product

Interfaces, prototypes, and design systems — judged on how it feels in someone else's hands, not just yours.

Node 05

Game Dev & XR

Real-time engines, shaders, and immersive worlds — playable builds only, no pitch decks.

Node 06

Security & CTF

Exploits, forensics, and defense under pressure. The scoreboard is public for the whole fest.

02 — schedule

Three days, laid out end to end.

Tap any point on the line for what's actually happening then — no separate PDF to hunt for.

Day one — Feb 20
09:00Gates open, registration desk live+
Badge pickup, team check-in, and bench assignment for all six tracks.
10:30Opening keynote+
"Systems that outlive their creators" — on building things meant to be maintained by someone else.
12:00All six tracks kick off+
Mentors introduced, hardware bench opens, problem statements released per track.
19:00Build night begins+
The 24-hour build clock starts. Bench and lounge stay open through the night.
Day two — Feb 21
13:00CTF finals+
Top eight teams from the qualifying scoreboard face off live on the main screen.
17:00Design crit + portfolio reviews+
Open review slots with visiting designers — bring work in progress, not just finished decks.
21:00Submissions lock+
Repos and demo builds freeze. No commits accepted past this point.
Day three — Feb 22
10:00Judging rounds+
Closed-door judging per track, five teams at a time, fifteen minutes each.
14:00Demo showcase+
Open to the public — every team demos at their bench, no stage required.
18:00Closing ceremony + awards+
Winners announced per track, plus one cross-track Best in Show.
